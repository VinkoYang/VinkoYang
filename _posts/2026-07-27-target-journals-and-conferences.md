# A Researcher's Guide to Target Journals and Conferences in HRI, AR/VR, and Smart Manufacturing

If you work at the intersection of **human-robot interaction (HRI), augmented/virtual reality (AR/VR), robot programming, digital twins, and smart manufacturing (Industry 4.0)**, the venue landscape can feel scattered — some venues live in the robotics world, some in HCI, some in mechanical/industrial engineering, and some in computer vision. Below is a curated, tiered list of journals and conferences relevant to this research area, with a short description of each venue's scope and a link to its official homepage. Useful as a quick-reference for literature reviews or when deciding where to submit next.

---

## Journals

### Tier 1 — Core Venues

**[Journal of Intelligent Manufacturing (JIM)](https://link.springer.com/journal/10845)**
Published by Springer, JIM is a leading outlet for applying AI and intelligent systems to manufacturing, covering topics like production planning, robotics in manufacturing, and cyber-physical systems.

**[ASME Journal of Computing and Information Science in Engineering (JCISE)](https://asmedigitalcollection.asme.org/computingengineering)**
An ASME journal focused on computational methods, information technology, AR/VR, and digital engineering tools used across the product design and manufacturing lifecycle.

**[Robotics and Computer-Integrated Manufacturing (RCIM)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/robotics-and-computer-integrated-manufacturing)**
An Elsevier journal dedicated to industrial robotics, automation, and computer-integrated manufacturing systems — a strong fit for human-robot collaboration and cobot research.

**[International Journal of Human-Computer Interaction (IJHCI)](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/hihc20)**
A Taylor & Francis journal covering the design, evaluation, and theory of human-computer interfaces, including AR/VR interaction and usability studies.

**[ASTM Smart and Sustainable Manufacturing Systems](https://www.astm.org/journals/smart-sustainable-manufacturing-systems)**
Focuses on smart manufacturing technologies, sustainability, and systems-level innovation in modern production environments.

---

### Tier 2 — High-Relevance Targets

**[IEEE Transactions on Human-Machine Systems (THMS)](https://cybernetics.ieee.org/publications/ieee-transactions-on-human-machine-systems)**
Covers human factors, human-machine collaboration, and interface design across robotics, automation, and cyber-physical systems.

**[IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L)](https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/ra-l)**
A fast-turnaround IEEE letters journal covering all areas of robotics and automation, often paired with presentation at IROS or ICRA.

**[ACM Transactions on Human-Robot Interaction (THRI)](https://dl.acm.org/journal/thri)**
The premier ACM journal specifically dedicated to HRI research, spanning social robotics, collaborative robots, and human-centered robot design.

**[International Journal of Social Robotics](https://www.springer.com/journal/12369)**
A Springer journal on social and assistive robotics, human-robot communication, and robot behavior design.

**[Computers in Industry](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computers-in-industry)**
Covers digital transformation of industry, including digital twins, Industry 4.0, and computer-based systems in manufacturing.

**[Journal of Manufacturing Systems](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-manufacturing-systems)**
An Elsevier journal on manufacturing systems design, automation, and smart factory technologies.

**[Virtual Reality (Springer)](https://www.springer.com/journal/10055)**
Dedicated to VR/AR technologies, systems, and applications, including tracking, calibration, and immersive interaction techniques.

**[IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics (TVCG)](https://www.computer.org/csdl/journal/tg)**
A top-tier venue for visualization, computer graphics, and AR/VR research, including the journal track associated with IEEE VR and ISMAR.

---

### Tier 3 — Extended / Cross-Disciplinary

**[International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology (IJAMT)](https://www.springer.com/journal/170)**
Covers manufacturing processes, technologies, and systems broadly, from machining to automation.

**[Sensors (MDPI)](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/sensors)**
An open-access journal covering sensing technologies, perception systems, and applications in robotics and human-machine interfaces.

**[Applied Ergonomics](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/applied-ergonomics)**
Focuses on human factors and ergonomics in workplace and system design, relevant to human-robot collaboration safety and usability.

**[Automation in Construction](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/automation-in-construction)**
Covers automation, robotics, and digital technologies (including AR/VR and digital twins) applied to the construction industry.

## Conferences

### Tier 1 — Core Venues

**[IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS)](https://www.iros.org)**
One of the two flagship international robotics conferences, covering all areas of intelligent robots and systems, from manipulation to human-robot interaction.

**[ASME International Manufacturing Science and Engineering Conference (MSEC)](https://event.asme.org/MSEC)**
ASME's flagship manufacturing conference, covering manufacturing processes, systems, robotics, and Industry 4.0 technologies.

**[ASME International Design Engineering Technical Conferences (IDETC-CIE)](https://event.asme.org/IDETC-CIE)**
Combines design engineering and computing/information science tracks, covering CAD, digital design tools, and AR/VR in engineering design.

**[ACM CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI)](https://sigchi.org/conferences/chi)**
The premier venue in human-computer interaction, covering interface design, usability, and interaction techniques including AR/VR and HRI.

**[IEEE International Conference on Robot and Human Interactive Communication (RO-MAN)](https://www.ieee-ras.org/conferences-workshops/technically-co-sponsored/ro-man)**
A dedicated conference for human-robot and human-machine interactive communication, social robotics, and cognitive robotics.

---

### Tier 2 — High-Priority Targets

**[ACM/IEEE International Conference on Human-Robot Interaction (HRI)](https://humanrobotinteraction.org)**
The top dedicated venue for HRI research, spanning technical, social, and design-oriented approaches to human-robot interaction.

**[IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA)](https://www.ieee-ras.org/conferences-workshops/fully-sponsored/icra)**
The other flagship robotics conference alongside IROS, covering the full breadth of robotics research.

**[IEEE International Symposium on Mixed and Augmented Reality (ISMAR)](https://www.ismar.net)**
The leading conference dedicated specifically to AR/MR technologies, tracking, registration, and applications.

**[IEEE Conference on Virtual Reality and 3D User Interfaces (IEEE VR)](https://ieeevr.org)**
IEEE's flagship VR conference, covering immersive technologies, 3D interaction, and virtual environments.

**[International Conference on Extended Reality (ICXR)](https://icxr.net)**
Organized by the China Computer Federation (CCF), covering VR, AR, and MR technologies, applications, and the emerging metaverse.

**[American Society for Engineering Education (ASEE) Annual Conference](https://www.asee.org)**
A major conference for engineering education research, including robotics/AR curriculum design and pedagogy.

---

### Tier 3 — Extended / AI & Vision Crossover

**[Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS)](https://neurips.cc)**
A top-tier AI/machine learning conference, relevant for research bridging AI methods (e.g., LLMs, prompt tuning) with robotics.

**[IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV)](https://iccv.thecvf.com)**
A premier computer vision conference, relevant to vision-based robot perception and vision-language-action (VLA) models.

**[IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR)](https://cvpr.thecvf.com)**
The largest computer vision conference, covering perception, recognition, and increasingly robotics-vision integration.

**[IEEE International Conference on Automation Science and Engineering (CASE)](https://www.ieeecase.org)**
Covers automation systems, robotics, and manufacturing automation from an industrial engineering perspective.

**[ACM Symposium on User Interface Software and Technology (UIST)](https://uist.acm.org)**
Covers novel interaction techniques and interface technologies, closely related to AR/VR interface research.
