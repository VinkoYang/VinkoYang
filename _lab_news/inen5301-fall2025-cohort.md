On December 7, 2025, students in **INEN-5301: Collaborative Robot Operations
and Programming** presented their final projects. Fall 2025 was the second time
the course was offered — first launched in Fall 2024 — and this cohort had 10
students enrolled, 7 of whom earned an **A**.

## The course

INEN-5301 is a hands-on introduction to programming and operating collaborative
robots. Students work directly on a **Universal Robots UR10** cobot and move
from basic jogging and waypoint teaching to sensor-driven automation: digital
and analog I/O, tool outputs, IF/ELSE decision logic, variable speed control,
conveyor integration, and coordinate frames (base, tool, and user frames).
Motion planning is covered through `MoveJ` and `MoveL` commands and
waypoint-based trajectory design. The emphasis throughout is on turning
robotics concepts into working automation routines.

## The individual projects

Each student completed two individual projects under the instructor's
supervision:

- **Task 1 — Pick-and-Place operation.** Programming the UR10 for automated
  material handling with conveyor integration and sensor feedback, using
  IF/ELSE logic, digital I/O, tool outputs, and waypoint programming to build a
  sensor-based automated cycle.
- **Task 2 — Writing Task with L-ID.** Programming the robot to write
  alphanumeric sequences using the TCP and a user frame, with `MoveJ` /
  `MoveL` commands and waypoint-based trajectory planning.
