On Monday, October 5, 2026, students in **INEN 6301: Advanced Robotics** were
back in the XRAI Lab for their next UR10e lab session. A week earlier the
[first race](/lab/news/advanced-robotics-cobot-lab-session/) used robot motion
only. This time the robot had to work with the rest of the cell through
**inputs and outputs (I/O)**.

## The challenge

Each group programmed the UR10e, the **conveyor**, and the cell's **presence
sensors** to run one loop:

1. **Sensor 1** detects the cube.
2. The robot program starts the conveyor and carries the cube to the
   **Sensor 3** position, where the conveyor stops.
3. The UR10e picks the cube up and returns it to Sensor 1.

The robot has to read the sensor inputs and switch the conveyor output in the
right order. Every handoff between the conveyor and the robot depends on the
signal timing.

## The winners

**Group 2** completed the task with the fastest cycle. Pictured in the front
row is **Sajita Shrestha**. In the back row, from right to left: **Aminat
Ayankoya**, **Vivian Latona**, **Rocktim Shil**, and **Adnan Shafi**.
