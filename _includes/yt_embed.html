{%- comment -%} YouTube embed that loads only a thumbnail until clicked (~4 MB of player JS per iframe otherwise). The iframe starts from a tiny srcdoc page showing the video's poster and a play link; clicking navigates the iframe to the real player with autoplay. Browsers without srcdoc support fall back to the normal src. Params: include.src (a youtube.com/embed/... URL), include.title. Non-YouTube URLs render as a plain lazy iframe. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign yt_src = include.src -%} {%- if yt_src contains "youtube.com/embed/" or yt_src contains "youtube-nocookie.com/embed/" -%} {%- assign yt_id = yt_src | split: "/embed/" | last | split: "?" | first -%} {%- if yt_src contains "?" -%}{%- assign yt_play = yt_src | append: "&autoplay=1" -%}{%- else -%}{%- assign yt_play = yt_src | append: "?autoplay=1" -%}{%- endif -%} {%- else -%} {%- endif -%}
