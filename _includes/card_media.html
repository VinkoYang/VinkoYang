{%- comment -%} Card thumbnail for a research item. Params: include.image (path under /images/), include.alt. Animated GIFs are 5–11 MB each, so a `.gif` is rendered as a muted looping video from the sibling `.mp4` / `.webm` with `-poster.jpg` (all generated from the GIF; keep the three next to it when adding a new one). No `autoplay` attribute: browsers often fetch autoplay video even with preload="none". site.js plays `data-inview-play` videos only while they are in the viewport, and leaves them on the poster under prefers-reduced-motion. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign media_url = include.image | prepend: '/images/' | prepend: site.baseurl | prepend: site.url -%} {%- if include.image contains ".gif" -%} {%- assign media_base = media_url | remove: ".gif" -%} {%- else -%} {%- endif -%}
